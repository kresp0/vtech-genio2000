# Photographs

All photographs are by the author (CC BY 4.0, see `../NOTICE.md`); VTech marks and artwork in them are not covered. Images were recompressed to at most 1600 px and their metadata removed.

* `unit/`: the Spanish Genio 2000 (closed and open, lid and LCD, keyboard and activity panel, the CONTRASTE slide switch and the caps-lock LED off and on, underside).
* `board/`: the opened unit and the main board (ROM, RAM and the CPU area).
* `cartridge/`: a "Super Memoria PC 2000 & Genio 2000" cartridge, outside and inside.

Captions are minimal on purpose: see `docs/hardware/HARDWARE.md` for what the parts are and how they were identified.
